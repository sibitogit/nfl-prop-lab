# FREE / PRO v1 product boundary

## Product principle
FREE must prove the core product is useful. PRO must save time and add research depth rather than merely remove an arbitrary paywall.

## FREE v1
Question answered: “How has this player prop performed recently?”

The user can:
- choose any supported player and market
- enter the sportsbook line manually
- choose Over or Under
- backtest the last five available regular-season games
- see hit rate, W-L-P, average, median, chart and underlying game log

## PRO v1
Question answered: “What happens when I research this prop more deeply?”

PRO adds:
- latest season, L10 and L20 samples
- sample-to-sample comparison
- nearby-line analysis and alternate-line matrix
- season-by-season history
- Home/Away and opponent splits
- Defense vs Position historical context
- P25/P50/P75, range and volatility
- expanded Research Summary
- CSV export

## Guardrails
- No model probability
- No expected value or “edge” score
- No wager recommendation
- No account, bankroll or payment information inside the research app
- Keep initial infrastructure cost at USD 0
